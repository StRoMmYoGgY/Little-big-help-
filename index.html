Cost Separation Standard

Five Transparent Cost Components

ALLORA never hides third-party expenses inside its service fees and never adds unapproved charges.

  • 1. ALLORA Service Fee: Agreed coordination, planning, or delivery fee in BDT.
  • 2. Transportation: Charged separately at agreed actual cost.
  • 3. Customer Purchases: Exact retail/product cost confirmed prior to buying.
  • 4. Third-Party Expenses: Venue tickets, domains, hosting, courier, printing, or external fees.
  • 5. Approved Scope Additions: Extra stops, waiting time, or expanded tasks approved by you first.

Advance & Reconciliation Policy

Clear Financial Handling Across Categories

  • Concierge & Local Assistance: Advance payment covers the quoted service fee plus estimated task expenses. Original receipts are shared and unused funds are returned or credited.
  • Business Solutions: Milestone or monthly payment schedule documented clearly in writing before onboarding.
  • Website Development: 60% advance prior to development start; 40% final balance due before launch or handover.
  • Availability & Coverage: Physical assistance operates in Sylhet (other cities only via confirmed local partner). Digital Solutions operate Bangladesh-wide.
